01 / LIVE RETIMING

Drag markers.
Retime live.

Spread two markers apart to slow a moment down. Bring them closer to speed it up. The keys between them follow along in real time.

  • Double-click or Ctrl+Click to place a marker.
  • Drag a span bar to move an entire segment.
  • Shift+Click to move multiple markers together.

One marker shifts the whole animation. Keys beyond the outer markers move along too.

02 / EASE CONTROLS

A little ease.
A lot more character.

The blue diamond places the halfway point of the motion exactly where you want it. The two handles shape each side independently.

  • Pull outward for an ease in or ease out.
  • Pull inward for a fast start or fast end.
  • See the affected section and its live percentage.

Each handle affects its own half. Zoom in on short spans to reveal the handles.

03 / SMOOTH ZONES

Keep the rhythm.
Lose the jolt.

A sudden speed change can feel like a sudden stop. Give it room to breathe with a green smooth zone around the marker.

  • Widen the zone to round off the speed change.
  • Smooth your most recent retime live.
  • Press S to auto-smooth every marker.

Smoothing rounds the timing transition. Wide zones can shift a marker slightly.

Ready to try it?
Which Blender versions does it work with?
Retime Tool requires Blender 5.2 or newer. It works in both the Graph Editor and the Dope Sheet. The current extension version is 2.4.2.
How do I install it?
Download the extension ZIP from Superhive, then drag and drop the ZIP into Blender and confirm the installation. It’s a Blender Extension, so keep the ZIP as downloaded. Start it from the Retime button in either editor’s header or the sidebar tab.
Which curves will it retime?
Every editable curve shown in the editor. Use Blender’s visibility filters, such as Only Show Selected, to choose what is included. Enable Selected Curves Only to narrow the scope further.
How do undo, Enter, and Esc work?

Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z step back and forward through retime drags, including keys inserted at markers. Right-click during a drag cancels only that drag.

Enter keeps your changes and exits, adding one step to Blender’s normal undo history. Esc resets the animation to how it was when you started, then exits.

Will my keys stay on whole frames?
Keys can land on subframes and are not rounded. Snap Markers on Frame is on by default, but it controls the markers, not rounding for every key. Turn it off when you want subframe marker timing.
What should I know about smoothing?

Smooth zones round off speed changes. Because of that rounding, a marker can land a fraction of a frame away from where you dropped it. Wide zones and large speed changes make this more noticeable.

Adjusting a zone right after a retime smooths that most recent retime live. After another retime, an undo, or inserted keys, the zone applies to your next retime instead. Press S or use the sidebar to auto-smooth all markers.

Why can’t I see the ease handles?
Ease handles need a span of about 60 pixels on screen to appear. Zoom in on a short span to give the handles enough space.
